In cluster 0, the most atypical case involved an appeal challenging the EIR certification of a major public transit project, which also involved street vacation, which is a rare request. 
In cluster 1, the most atypical case involved a Writ of Mandamus issued by the Los Angeles Superior Court ordering the City to set aside a previously approved housing development because it hadn't adequately demonstrated TOC eligibility. However, the Writ did not limit the discretion vested in the City as to how to proceed, which is why the case is before the CPC.
In cluster 2, the most atypical case involved an appeal challenging a previous demolition approval of a single duplex, on the grounds of historical preservation. The small scale of the project combined with the rarity of the challenge type is what makes it unique.
